My commitments

Work, child care and elder care take time away from study. If you tell us the weekly hours, your score is multiplied by a small capped factor so a strong result alongside those hours is recognised. It is optional, and there is nothing to type but hours.

Before you start

Your hours are seen only by you and your instructor. Classmates see your final adjusted score and rank, never your hours, your factor or your raw score. The instructor approves your baseline before it counts. Everything you change is recorded in a log the instructor can review.
